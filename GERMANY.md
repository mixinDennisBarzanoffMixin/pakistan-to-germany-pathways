# Germany: official work and training routes

Rules checked 10 October 2026. Salary and funding thresholds change, often yearly.

## 1. Direct qualified job

The [work visa for qualified professionals (§18b)](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/work-qualified-professionals) needs a German-comparable academic degree and a specific qualified job offer. The job may be outside the exact degree field, but it must normally require a degree or qualified vocational training. **Auxiliary jobs such as cleaning do not qualify.** This is the most direct route if an employer offers engineering, electrical design, power systems, automation, testing, maintenance engineering, commissioning, or another qualifying role. The mission's [Pakistan employment page](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2595682-2595682) calls for the contract and degree evidence.

German is not a statutory visa requirement for this category, according to the [federal language overview](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/living-in-germany/learn-german/knowledge), but the employer can require it. English-only roles are a narrower search.

## 2. EU Blue Card

The [Blue Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card) needs a comparable degree, a job offer of at least six months that matches the qualification, and a salary floor. For **2026**, the general threshold is **€50,700 gross/year**. The lower **€45,934.20** threshold can apply to shortage occupations including academic STEM professionals, or to new labour-market entrants whose last degree was obtained less than three years ago; Federal Employment Agency approval may be needed. Verify the exact job classification and current threshold before relying on this route. A qualified offer below the Blue Card floor may still fit the ordinary §18b skilled-worker route.

## 3. Opportunity Card

The [official Opportunity Card guide](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) has two branches:

- **Recognised-qualification branch:** a degree comparable to a German one. No points or German-language proof for this branch.
- **Points branch:** a state-recognised foreign qualification, German A1 **or** English B2, and at least six points. At age 25, the age factor gives two points; do not assume the remaining points without checking each criterion in the official calculator.

Both branches require funding. The federal 2026 figure is **€1,091 net/month** through a blocked account or declaration of commitment. The card is normally issued for up to one year. It permits a total of **20 hours/week** of side work and trial work up to two weeks per employer. Part-time cleaning could help with living costs under this permit; it is **not** a route to an unrestricted full-time cleaning job. To remain for work, obtain a qualifying job and switch to the appropriate residence title. See the [federal Q&A](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers).

The [Karachi mission page](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2632954-2632954) currently lists an older-looking **€1,027/month** and €500 for a return ticket, and says Islamabad uses the Consular Services Portal. Resolve this discrepancy against the live application requirements before depositing funds.

## 4. Vocational training (Ausbildung)

The [training visa (§16a)](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/training) needs a specific training place and normally **B1 German**. In 2026, living costs are considered covered for company-based training when remuneration reaches **€1,048 gross / €822 net monthly**; a shortfall can be topped up. School-based training needs **€959 net/month** via accepted funding. Technical trades, electrical installation, mechatronics, and industrial maintenance can be searched, but confirm the employer and school language requirements.

The same official page describes a **training-place search visa** for applicants under 35 with B1 German, a qualifying school certificate, and **€1,091/month** funding in 2026. It permits up to nine months of search and 20 hours/week work. At 25, the age criterion fits; the others still need proof.

## Applying from Pakistan

For ordinary building-site work (`Bauhelfer`), see [the construction-specific route comparison](BAUSTELLE.md), including short-term quota employment and its employer-paid travel requirement.

Use the [German mission's national-visa category pages](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106) and the linked Consular Services Portal or Karachi instructions, according to residence/jurisdiction. Gather passport, degree and transcripts, recognition evidence, CV, offer/training contract or Opportunity Card documents, finance and insurance evidence, and any required translations/attestations **according to the exact live checklist**. The mission's [Opportunity Card checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2660740/fbda69ed9a7f88712ee01d161e67cff9/opportunity-card-pdf-data.pdf) refers to HEC-attested degree/transcript. Do not assume a processing time; the mission controls appointments and decisions.
