# Erasmus Mundus and the UAE: decision sheet

Checked 10 October 2026 for the Rawalpindi applicant (BE electrical engineering, C1 English, little money).

## Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

The [European Commission's student guide](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters) says applicants worldwide may apply directly to a chosen programme. The common baseline is a **bachelor's degree**, completed before the master's starts. His Air University BE meets that degree-level starting point; each programme decides whether the subject, grades and other evidence fit. Typical applications are **October–January** for the following academic year. This is a master's course, usually one or two years, involving study in multiple countries; it is not an unrestricted work visa or guaranteed permanent residence.

Each programme publishes its own admission rules. Prepare: passport, degree and transcript, English-language evidence in the form that specific programme accepts, CV, motivation statement, and whatever references or project evidence that programme asks for. **Self-reported C1 is useful but may not replace an accepted IELTS/TOEFL or other documented proof.** Search the [official catalogue](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters) for electrical power, energy systems, electronics, automation, embedded systems, semiconductors and related subjects. Check each course's participating countries; an Erasmus award does not guarantee Ireland.

The [2026 Erasmus programme rules](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-2/erasmus-mundus) say selected projects cannot charge student application fees, and scholarship holders cannot be charged tuition or mandatory participation costs. EU scholarship support is calculated at **€1,400/month** for the programme duration, intended to cover travel, visa, arrival and living costs. Check when the first payment arrives; he may still need cash for passport, language test, visa appointment or initial travel before reimbursement/payment.

**Acceptance rate:** no official single applicant-to-scholarship percentage is published for all Erasmus Mundus master's programmes. Programme-level applicant counts and awards differ; EU figures on how many *programmes* were selected cannot be used as a *student* acceptance rate. Treat it as highly competitive and apply to several suitable programmes rather than planning around an invented percentage. The award is for the best-ranked applicants, per the [Commission's student guide](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters).

## Is the UAE better than Pakistan for him?

**Potentially as an earnings-and-exit step, conditional on the actual contract.** The [UAE government](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/tips-to-avoid-labour-and-visa-fraud) says a visitor visa does not permit work and the employer is responsible for recruitment expenses. Its [worker brochure](https://u.ae/-/media/Documents-2021/MOHRE_Brochure_A6_V4_Preview-GP-comments-%282%29.pdf) says employer costs include the entry visa and travel. This could reduce his immediate cash barrier. But the UAE [has no general minimum salary in its labour law](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/payment-of-wages); low-paid offers can leave almost no savings after food, housing and transport. Formal wages should be paid through the Wage Protection System, but [ILO supervisory material](https://normlex.ilo.org/dyn/nrmlx_es/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A13100%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A%3AP13100_COMMENT_ID%2CP13100_LANG_CODE%3A4417968%2Cen) highlights continuing concerns about fees, job transfer and passport retention in practice. Ordinary employment residence depends on having valid status; it is not a direct European-style settlement path.

Do **not** decide from the country name alone. Before accepting any UAE offer, get the written monthly salary, overtime terms, hours, actual worksite, accommodation details/photos, food/transport provision, medical insurance, employer-paid flight/visa, and termination/return travel terms. Compare **expected monthly savings** (`salary + guaranteed cash allowances − food − transport − housing − debt repayments`) with his realistic Pakistan option. If the recruiter wants large fees, the role differs from the signed offer, or housing/pay is vague, reject it. Aim for electrical technician or maintenance work before generic labour if available; it preserves a stronger career path.

## Recommendation

Apply to relevant Erasmus Mundus programmes for the **2027 intake** as the no-employer European route, while seeking **verified, employer-funded UAE jobs** for a nearer-term earnings route. Neither route is guaranteed. The UAE is preferable only when the specific job produces safe, positive net savings and he can keep control of his documents and exit options.
