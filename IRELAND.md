# Ireland for this applicant

Checked 10 October 2026. Pakistani citizen, 25, BE electrical engineering, C1 English, little money. **Ireland is a serious English-speaking settlement option if an eligible employer hires him from abroad.** An [employment permit comes before the employment visa](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/employment-visa/). There is no general visa to arrive and work at any job.

| Route | Target jobs | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Critical Skills Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/) | Electrical engineer; [explicitly on the critical-skills list](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/highly-skilled-eligible-occupations-list/critical%20skills%20occupations%20list.html) | Relevant degree, **two-year offer**, salary at least **€40,904** in 2026; no Labour Market Needs Test. His Nov 2024 graduation is too old for the 12-month Irish-graduate reduction. Actual job skills still matter. |
| [General Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/) | Horticulture worker, meat processing operative, healthcare assistant/home support worker; also other eligible qualified jobs | Real offer, eligible occupation, usually Labour Market Needs Test. 2026 salary floor **€32,691** for those named lower-threshold roles and **€36,605** for ordinary GEP roles. Healthcare jobs may require relevant training/experience. |

**Do not target cleaner or ordinary construction labourer for an Irish permit.** Both are on the [2026 ineligible occupations list](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/ineligible-categories-of-employment/ineligible%20list%20of%20occupations%20.html). These roles cannot be his first permit route under normal circumstances.

## Why Ireland is worth applying to

The English-language advantage is real, and the EE degree fits an occupation the government explicitly lists. Critical Skills is the best durable path: after **21 months** of qualifying employment, he may be eligible to upgrade to Stamp 4; General Employment Permit holders may be eligible after **57 months**. Stamp 4 allows work without an employment permit, subject to its conditions. These are conditional milestones, not automatic approvals. [Irish immigration's Stamp 4 guidance](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/how-to-renew-your-current-permission/information-on-stamp-4-upgrades/).

## How to try from Pakistan

1. Make two honest CV versions: **electrical engineering** (projects, tools, commissioning/maintenance/design experience) and **eligible practical work** (horticulture/meat processing/healthcare only if skills fit). Do not claim paid experience or care credentials he lacks.
2. Search the [Irish public jobs service](https://www.jobsireland.ie/) and direct employers for `graduate electrical engineer`, `junior electrical engineer`, `electrical design engineer`, `horticulture worker`, and `meat processing operative`. Check the advertised salary and ask if the employer considers non-EEA applicants needing a permit. Prioritise the EE applications; a degree alone does not exempt him from a job offer.
3. For a GEP, ask if the employer will complete the [Labour Market Needs Test](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/) and apply. An application normally needs the job advertised through DSP/EURES and another online platform before submission. The first permit costs [€1,000 for up to two years](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/fees/); ask the employer to apply/pay, and separately ask about flight and initial housing. When the employer is the permit applicant, it cannot deduct or recover the permit application expenses from the worker under the [official GEP rules](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/).
4. After permit approval, apply for the [long-stay employment visa](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/employment-visa/) from Pakistan, then travel. Do not buy a ticket on an interview promise.

**Funding reality:** unlike the UAE, Ireland has no blanket rule here that the employer pays the flight. He must negotiate relocation help. With essentially no money and no offer, Ireland is an application campaign, not an immediate departure ticket. The 2025 Irish seasonal permit was a [pilot](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/publications/seasonal-employment-permit-pilot-scheme-2025-evaluation-report.html); we have not verified an open 2026 no-offer intake.
