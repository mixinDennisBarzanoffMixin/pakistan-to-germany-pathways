# 30-day action plan

**Current constraint: no savings.** Lead with verified employers and official Pakistan recruitment channels. Keep engineering applications as an additional route, not the only route. See [ROUTE_MATRIX.md](ROUTE_MATRIX.md).

## Days 1–3: establish the facts

- Confirm the exact diploma title and awarding campus. Air University, Islamabad already shows H+ in anabin; the generic Pakistani four-year Bachelor of [Fachrichtung] Engineering shows Entspricht. Save both official printouts if the diploma matches. Start ZAB if the exact match cannot be shown.
- Record HEC/PEC status, years and type of paid work, English test evidence, savings, passport validity, and whether there is a sponsor in Germany. Use [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) privately.
- Rawalpindi falls under the German Embassy Islamabad. Read its [employment/jobseeker national-visa page](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106) and start through the linked Consular Services Portal once the visa branch is determined.
- Confirm passport validity, CNIC, contact details, and whether an employer can pay the entire recruitment/travel process. Do not upload personal documents publicly.

## Days 4–10: prepare applications

- Make a one-page English CV with actual electrical projects, software, lab equipment, commissioning/testing experience, and measurable outcomes. Create a German-language version as ability improves; never invent experience.
- Prepare a portfolio of 2–3 real projects, redacting private employer material. Keep passport and personal documents outside this public repository.
- Search [BEOE foreign jobs](https://beoe.gov.pk/foreign-jobs) and [OEC](https://jobs.oec.gov.pk/home/index.html) for UAE/Saudi jobs, including entry-level work; verify any intermediary on the [BEOE licensed promoter list](https://beoe.gov.pk/index.php/list-of-oeps). Ask for a signed offer stating salary, hours, housing, travel, and fees. Search Malta/Poland employers as a second track.
- Search [Make it in Germany job listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings/job-listings), [Federal Employment Agency advice](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job/looking-for-job), and employers directly for EE roles. Search electrical engineer, power systems, automation, controls, test engineer, commissioning engineer, and maintenance engineer.
- Search [JobsIreland](https://jobsireland.ie/en-US/job-seekers) and Irish employers for electrical and controls engineering roles that meet Critical Skills conditions. Add UK roles through [GOV.UK Find a Job](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-job), checking each employer against the [licensed sponsor register](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers).

## Days 11–30: run the routes in parallel

- Send tailored applications to qualified jobs and log each role, sponsor/contact, salary, date, and response in a **private** tracker. Ask employers whether they hire from Pakistan and support the work-permit process.
- For UK offers, ask the sponsor to confirm SOC code 2123 (or the actual role code) and whether the under-26 new-entrant rule can be used. Start evidence for UK B2 English through an approved test or Ecctis if needed.
- Start German study immediately. A1/A2 improves daily life and the points branch; B1 opens the training route. Use an accepted certificate provider if proof is needed; see the [federal language guidance](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/living-in-germany/learn-german/knowledge).
- Do not assemble a self-funded Opportunity Card application while there are no funds. Revisit it only if a German resident can formally sponsor him, a qualifying 20-hour contract covers the support amount, or he saves enough. Do not count on finding cleaning work after arrival to satisfy the pre-visa proof requirement.
- Ask every prospective employer whether it pays or advances visa fees, travel, and other required costs. The legal employer-pays rule is strongest for the UAE; confirm the actual written terms in every country.

## Decision points

- **Recognised degree + qualified offer:** German §18b; compare Blue Card salary.
- **Recognised degree + sufficient funds/sponsor/qualifying part-time contract, no qualified offer:** Opportunity Card.
- **Degree not comparable:** resolve through ZAB; separately assess the points branch on official criteria.
- **B1 German + training offer:** vocational training visa.
- **Irish two-year EE offer:** Critical Skills permit and then employment visa.
- **No funds / no offer / no B1:** seek verified employer-paid UAE/Saudi recruitment and employer-sponsored Malta/Poland/UK seasonal options from Pakistan; build skills and savings while applying. There is no general “arrive and take any job” visa in these researched routes.
