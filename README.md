# Legal routes from Pakistan to Germany (and alternatives)

Research snapshot: **10 October 2026**. Profile: Pakistani citizen, age 25, Air University BE in electrical engineering (Sep 2019–Nov 2024), C1 English, no German yet, based in Rawalpindi. See the [case status](CASE_STATUS.md) for what is verified and what still blocks a visa application. This is a planning guide, not a visa decision. Verify every rule and amount on the linked official page immediately before applying.

## The short answer

**Best first move with no substantial savings:** use the [country route matrix](ROUTE_MATRIX.md) to pursue verified **UAE and Saudi employer-paid recruitment** first. He may be able to raise small amounts after a genuine offer, so also ask Maltese, Polish, and UK seasonal employers exactly which costs they pay or advance. His BE and English justify applications for better-paid engineering jobs in Germany, Ireland, and the UK. He can seek nonengineering work in several countries, but each visa still has a job/permit gate. Do not pay a recruiter for a promise of a visa.

Germany's [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) is **conditional** because small amounts for fees or travel would not satisfy its living-cost requirement. With a German-comparable degree, its skilled-worker branch does **not** require German proof or points, but it is a job-search permit with work limited to 20 hours a week. In 2026 he must show **€1,091 net per month** of living-cost support, through his own blocked account, an eligible German resident's formal declaration of commitment, or qualifying part-time pay. Twelve months is **€13,092** of his own support money, not a German government grant. A shorter card may be possible with proportionately less support. See [what proof of funds means](FUNDING.md).

If degree comparability, funds, or a job offer is missing, start German now and consider [paid vocational training](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/training). This generally needs **B1 German**, a training place, and adequate income/funds. It is a medium-term route, not an immediate way to enter for any job.

## Choose a route

The [country route matrix](ROUTE_MATRIX.md) covers the realistic nonengineering and engineering options across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malta, Poland, Czechia, Ireland, Germany, the UK, Canada, Japan, South Korea, New Zealand, Australia, and Croatia. It distinguishes a temporary exit from a durable relocation path.

| Route | Gate to clear | Fit for this profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [German skilled-worker visa](GERMANY.md#1-direct-qualified-job) | Comparable degree and a concrete **qualified** job offer | Best if the degree checks out and an employer hires him. No statutory German requirement for this visa, though employers may require it. |
| [EU Blue Card](GERMANY.md#2-eu-blue-card) | Matching qualified offer and salary threshold | Strong if salary qualifies; harder for a new graduate. |
| [Opportunity Card](GERMANY.md#3-opportunity-card) | Recognition **or** points; living costs funded | Useful for on-site job search; part-time auxiliary work is allowed within its limit, but does not itself create a long-term work route. |
| [Vocational training](GERMANY.md#4-vocational-training-ausbildung) | Training place, usually B1 German, livelihood | Practical reset if willing to learn German. |
| [Ireland Critical Skills](ALTERNATIVES.md#ireland) | Two-year electrical-engineering offer and salary floor | Main English-speaking backup. |
| [UK Skilled Worker](ALTERNATIVES.md#united-kingdom) | Licensed sponsor, eligible EE offer, salary and B2 English proof | Under-26 new-entrant salary rule makes it worth applying now. |
| [Canada Express Entry](ALTERNATIVES.md#canada) | Skilled work experience, test scores, credential assessment, competitive ranking | Longer-term backup if experience and funds exist. |

## Start here

1. Read [degree recognition](RECOGNITION.md). Air University, Islamabad is listed **H+** in anabin, and anabin lists a Pakistani four-year “Bachelor of [Fachrichtung] Engineering” as **Entspricht**. Confirm his diploma's exact award title and campus against those entries, then save official printouts. **HEC/PEC certification alone does not prove German comparability.**
2. Fill out the [personal decision sheet](CHECKLIST.md) without committing sensitive documents to this repository.
3. Follow the [30-day action plan](ACTION_PLAN.md): start with employer-funded overseas roles, keep engineering applications in parallel, and prepare the correct visa branch.
4. Use the [official source index](SOURCES.md) for current rules. The German mission's Karachi Opportunity Card page currently quotes **€1,027/month**, while the federal portal quotes **€1,091/month for 2026**; the mission says Islamabad applications use the Consular Services Portal. Treat the amount as unresolved until the current application checklist confirms it.

**Important:** A tourist/Schengen visa is not permission to take a job. No agent can guarantee a visa or buy a faster German embassy appointment. Use official portals and keep originals and payments under your control. The [German mission in Pakistan warns about fraudulent appointment agents](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/visa-shortterm-1673126).
