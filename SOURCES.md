# Official sources and review notes

Checked **10 October 2026**. Links below are official government/agency sources. Rules, fees, and amounts must be rechecked at application time.

## Germany

- [Qualified professional work visa](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/work-qualified-professionals) — comparable qualification, qualified job, auxiliary-work exclusion.
- [EU Blue Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card) — job match and 2026 salary thresholds.
- [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) and [Q&A](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers) — two eligibility branches, funding, permitted side work.
- [Vocational training visa](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/training) — B1, training contract, 2026 support figures, training-search visa.
- [Language requirements](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/living-in-germany/learn-german/knowledge) — visa-specific statutory requirements and accepted certificates.
- [anabin institutions](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen) (Air University, Islamabad H+ as checked 10 October 2026), [anabin degrees](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/hochschulabschluesse) (Pakistani four-year Bachelor of [Fachrichtung] Engineering: Entspricht), [official guide](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/pdfs/guide-anabin), [ZAB comparability](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-comparability), [Recognition Finder](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/2722.php).
- [German mission Pakistan: employment](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2595682-2595682), [work/jobseeker categories](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106), [Karachi Opportunity Card page](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2632954-2632954) and [checklist PDF](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2660740/fbda69ed9a7f88712ee01d161e67cff9/opportunity-card-pdf-data.pdf).
- [Official job listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings/job-listings) and [job search guidance](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job/looking-for-job).

**Known discrepancy:** the Karachi Opportunity Card page says €1,027 monthly; the current federal page says €1,091 for 2026. The federal page is newer. Confirm with the consular portal/checklist before paying or depositing.
