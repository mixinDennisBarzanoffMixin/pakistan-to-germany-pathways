## Funding and staying abroad

A **full living-cost scholarship** is the key to avoiding a German blocked account. Germany also accepts a formal declaration of commitment from a qualified sponsor resident in Germany. Admission or a tuition waiver by itself does not establish living-cost funding. See [the German mission's Pakistan guidance](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/1995528-1995528). The 2026 blocked-account figure is €11,904, but a qualifying scholarship can replace that form of proof. For Erasmus programmes, the visa rules of the **first study country** also control; neither a scholarship nor a mobility semester guarantees a German post-study permit.
