# Staying after an Erasmus Mundus master's

Checked 10 October 2026. A scholarship and student residence permission do not create an automatic right to settle anywhere in Europe. Apply for the next residence title before the current one expires. The applicable country and degree depend on the actual mobility path.

## Germany

Graduates of a German university may apply for a residence permit for **up to 18 months** to find qualified employment. Germany requires proof of graduation, health insurance, and a secured livelihood. During this search period, the graduate may work **in any occupation**, including temporary helper work. A longer-term skilled-worker title or EU Blue Card requires a qualifying job and its own conditions. The 18-month search title is not renewable. See [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-vocational-training/studies-in-germany/prospects-after).

For E-PiCo+, a Kiel master's is possible **depending on the assigned mobility path**. The programme says scholarship holders' first-choice path is not guaranteed. Confirm with Kiel's international office and the local immigration authority whether the assigned path, German degree, and residence status qualify for the German graduate route. Sources: [E-PiCo+ degrees](https://master-epico.ec-nantes.fr/english-version/programme/degrees), [mobility assignment](https://master-epico.ec-nantes.fr/english-version/e-pico-programme).

## France

Graduates with an eligible French master's or equivalent may apply for the **12-month** residence permit for job search or business creation, subject to the French requirements. See [Service Public](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F17319). Employment on that permit must relate to the graduate's training or research and meet the current salary threshold; cleaning or construction helper work does not provide the same fallback as on Germany's graduate search permit. See [French work authorization rules](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2728).

## Practical plan

1. Before accepting an offer, ask E-PiCo+ which national degrees and residence documents the proposed mobility path will produce.
2. If Germany is the desired destination, request Kiel placement and get written advice from Kiel on post-study eligibility. The request does not guarantee placement.
3. During the master's, seek internships and qualified electrical, power electronics, EV, or controls employers. Learn the local language alongside the English-taught course.
4. Several months before graduation, check the applicable immigration authority's documents and submit the post-study application while current status remains valid.
5. Budget for the transition: the scholarship ends, and Germany requires proof of living costs even though the search permit allows any job.

If no further valid residence basis is obtained by the relevant deadline, the graduate must leave that country. A permit from one European country does not itself authorize residence and work in every other country.
