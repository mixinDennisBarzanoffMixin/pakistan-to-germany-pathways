**Recommendation with little money:** begin with verified UAE and Saudi employers where law places recruitment/permit costs on the employer. Also approach Maltese, Polish, and UK seasonal employers, **asking which unavoidable application and travel costs they cover or advance**. Apply to Irish/UK/German engineering jobs as a stronger-skills parallel track; a genuine employer offer may be more valuable than trying to fund a job-search visa. Germany's self-funded Opportunity Card is not ready unless a resident sponsor or qualifying part-time contract can satisfy the funding rule. None of these permits can be obtained merely by promising to work hard; a specific legal category and its evidence are required.

## Details and official application path

### Malta Single Permit

The [Maltese government says a third-country national with a job offer from a registered employer can receive a Single Permit](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/who-is-eligible/). The [employer submits the application](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/single-permit-application/) while the applicant is abroad; a first permit currently costs **€600**. From March 2026, a [pre-departure course](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/expatriates-unit-single-permit-pre-departure-course/) is required for first applicants. Tourism/hospitality can require an additional Skills Pass, but the [official FAQ](https://identita.gov.mt/frequently-asked-questions/expatriates/non-eu-employment/) describes different requirements for non-front-facing cleaners, dishwashers, and kitchen helpers. The [document list](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/documents-required/) includes proof of an employer vacancy advertisement. Get a written job offer and clarify who pays each fee and what happens if the job ends.

### UAE work permit

The UAE [work-permit rules](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/job-offers-and-work-permits-and-contracts/work-permits) allow employers to bring foreign workers for specified jobs. Verify the written offer and permit through official channels. The UAE government's [workers' rights page](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/labour-rights) says the employer bears recruitment, **travel**, and residency-permit costs. Its [fraud guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/tips-to-avoid-labour-and-visa-fraud) warns that working on a tourist visa is illegal. On the Pakistan side, use [BEOE foreign jobs](https://beoe.gov.pk/foreign-jobs) and the [government OEC job portal](https://jobs.oec.gov.pk/home/index.html), check any intermediary on the [licensed promoter list](https://beoe.gov.pk/index.php/list-of-oeps), and complete Protector/e-Protector requirements before departure. English can help, but the job and employer decide the actual language requirement.

### Saudi employer work visa

Saudi Arabia's [rules for employing non-Saudis](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/bn/node/5575987) require ministry approval, a written fixed-term contract, and a work permit for an approved occupation. The employer bears recruitment, residence and work-permit fees. The rules explicitly mention the worker's **return ticket** at the end of the contract; confirm who pays the inbound flight, Pakistan-side documents, and any medical tests in the written offer. Search [BEOE foreign jobs](https://beoe.gov.pk/foreign-jobs) and [OEC](https://jobs.oec.gov.pk/home/index.html), and verify any recruiter license. A Saudi work visa is tied to the approved job; no visitor-visa workaround.

### Poland work visa

A Polish employer must obtain the formal work permit, then he registers himself for a national **D work visa** through [Poland's Embassy in Islamabad and e-Konsulat](https://www.gov.pl/web/pakistan-en/d-type-national-visa). The official checklist asks for the original permit, employer invitation, experience/skills evidence, bank statements, insurance, accommodation, and other documents. Appointments can be allocated by lottery; [the embassy says it does not work with agents](https://www.gov.pl/web/pakistan-en/notice-on-new-requirements-for-registration-of-visa-appointments). This is a route for an actual employer, not an open job-search visa.

### Czech Employee Card

The [Czech Ministry of Foreign Affairs Employee Card guidance](https://mzv.gov.cz/jnp/en/information_for_aliens/long_stay_visa/employment_card.mobi?force_format=mobile) requires a specific contract and a vacancy registered in the central job database, along with the assigned vacancy number. It can cover jobs outside engineering if the vacancy and applicant meet the rules. It is normally valid for up to two years and can be renewed. Confirm embassy filing instructions and any job-specific language requirements before relying on an offer.

### Ireland General and Critical Skills

Ireland's [General Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/) can cover jobs outside engineering if they are not on the [ineligible list](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/ineligible-categories-of-employment/ineligible%20list%20of%20occupations%20.html), generally after a labour-market needs test. The 2026 standard floor is **€36,605**; lower figures apply to specified roles such as horticulture, meat processing, and care. **Cleaners are ineligible.** His electrical degree also makes [Critical Skills](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/) worth pursuing for a matching two-year offer at the current **€40,904** threshold. Permit approval must precede the [Irish employment visa](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/employment-visa/).

### Germany Opportunity Card

His Air University institution is H+ in [anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen), and the generic Pakistani four-year engineering bachelor's entry is “Entspricht” in the [degree database](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/hochschulabschluesse). The actual diploma wording/campus must be checked. If the degree is accepted as comparable, the [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search) does not require points or German proof. It allows job search and up to **20 hours/week** of side work in any lawful occupation. The 2026 living-cost benchmark is **€1,091/month**, usually shown through his own blocked account, a German resident's formal commitment, or qualifying part-time wages. The [federal Q&A](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers) says a shorter card may be issued if he can fund less than 12 months. This is not a grant from Germany; see [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md). A simple full-time cleaning job cannot by itself convert the card into a long-term work permit.

### UK Seasonal Worker

The [UK Seasonal Worker visa](https://www.gov.uk/seasonal-worker-visa/overview) permits sponsored horticulture work for up to six months, or poultry work during its specified autumn-to-December season. [Eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/seasonal-worker-visa/eligibility) includes a sponsor certificate and usually **£1,270** maintenance held for 28 days, unless the sponsor certifies support. The current visa fee is **£340**; airfare and other costs still need to be covered. It is temporary and does not lead to settlement or allow a second job. He must use an approved scheme operator; a random farm's verbal offer is insufficient. Ordinary UK cleaning is not a Skilled Worker occupation, and overseas sponsorship for care-worker roles closed in 2025: [eligible occupation list](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/skilled-worker-visa-eligible-occupations/skilled-worker-visa-eligible-occupations-and-codes), [care-worker guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/applying-for-health-and-social-care-jobs-in-the-uk-from-abroad/part-1-applying-for-health-and-social-care-jobs-in-the-uk-from-abroad).

## Routes not to rely on

- **Tourist/Schengen entry for work:** visiting does not authorize employment.
- **UK care worker from abroad:** [new overseas applications closed](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/applying-for-health-and-social-care-jobs-in-the-uk-from-abroad/part-1-applying-for-health-and-social-care-jobs-in-the-uk-from-abroad) in 2025.
- **UK Youth Mobility / common working-holiday schemes:** a Pakistani passport is not on the [UK list](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-youth-mobility-scheme-eligible-nationals) or [Canada IEC partner list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/iec/eligibility.html).
- **Portugal's former general job-seeker visa:** [current government policy](https://portugal.gov.pt/en/gc25/communication/news/regulated-and-humane-immigration-government-migration-policy-is-controlled-dignified-and-with-inte) restricts it to highly qualified applicants.
- **Spain's post-study job-search residence:** [the official route](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/migraciones/w/20.-autorizacion-de-residencia-para-busqueda-de-empleo-o-inicio-de-proyecto-empresarial) is for graduates of Spanish higher education, not this Pakistani degree alone.
