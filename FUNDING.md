# What “proof of funds” means

**Correction:** “Federal funding” was an inaccurate phrase. Germany is **not offering him a government grant** for the Opportunity Card. He must show that *he* can support himself while looking for a job.

For the German [Opportunity Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/job-search), the published **2026 benchmark is €1,091 net per month**. Twelve months is **€13,092**. This is a visa support requirement, not a fee paid to Germany. The [Federal Foreign Office explains blocked accounts](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/sperrkonto-388600): the money remains his, but monthly withdrawals are limited so it lasts through the stay. The embassy or immigration authority named as beneficiary **cannot withdraw it**. Provider fees, travel, insurance, and housing deposits are additional costs.

The [federal Q&A](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers) gives alternatives: a formal **Declaration of Commitment** by an eligible resident in Germany, or a German part-time job contract whose net pay covers some/all of the monthly amount. If he can fund less than 12 months, a **shorter-duration card may be possible**. Check the live [Islamabad online visa application](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106) for the exact evidence accepted. Its older Karachi page quotes a different monthly amount; do not rely on that figure for Islamabad.

Other countries use different concepts:

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Visa or permit **fee** | Payment to the government; generally not returned if refused. |
| **Maintenance** / proof of funds | Money he must show he has for living costs; sometimes a sponsor can certify support. |
| **Blocked account** | His own money placed in a restricted account, released in monthly amounts. |
| **Employer sponsorship** | A real employer's legal application/approval; it does **not** mean the worker is guaranteed a job or that all costs are free. |
| **Bank statements** | Evidence of genuine finances, often requested even when a job offer exists. |

**Current case:** he has no savings, so the self-funded Opportunity Card is not realistic now. It could become viable with a qualified German resident's formal commitment or a pre-arranged part-time contract whose accepted net income meets the visa requirement. Until then, prioritize genuine employer-offer routes and keep building savings.
