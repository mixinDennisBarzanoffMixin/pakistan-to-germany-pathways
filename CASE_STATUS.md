## First route decision

1. **Confirm exact diploma and anabin match.** If accepted as comparable, the Opportunity Card's recognised-degree branch is available without points or German proof, subject to funds and normal visa requirements. A qualified German offer would instead support the skilled-worker visa.
2. **Check funds.** A full 12-month Opportunity Card at the published benchmark implies €13,092 for living costs, plus other application and travel costs. A shorter requested stay may change the total required; the live consular checklist controls. A formal declaration of commitment is an alternative if available.
3. **If recognition cannot be proven:** the points route looks short **on currently known facts**: 2 for age + 1 for C1 English if certified + potentially 1 for an engineering shortage qualification = **up to 4 of 6**. Documented qualifying experience, German at B1, partial recognition, or another valid factor could change that. Do not file on an assumed score.
