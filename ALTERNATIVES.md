# English-speaking alternatives

Checked 10 October 2026. These are skilled routes; willingness to do any work does not remove their permit rules.

## Ireland

**Best parallel target.** Electrical engineers are on Ireland's [Critical Skills Occupations List](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/highly-skilled-eligible-occupations-list/critical%20skills%20occupations%20list.html). A [Critical Skills Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/) generally needs a two-year job offer, relevant qualifications/skills, and the applicable salary floor. The official [2026 remuneration schedule](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/economic-migration-policy/minimum-annual-remuneration/) gives **€40,904** from March 2026 for the relevant Critical Skills threshold; check the particular role and live page. The permit fee is **€1,000**. After permit approval, a Pakistani national must follow [Irish employment visa requirements](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/frequently-asked-questions-for-working-in-ireland/). Search EE, power, controls, automation, and commissioning roles. A cleaner job is not a Critical Skills engineering offer.

## Canada

[Federal Skilled Worker Express Entry](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/federal-skilled-workers.html) can be explored if he has **at least one year of continuous qualifying skilled work**, an approved language test at **CLB 7** or better, an Educational Credential Assessment for the foreign degree, enough eligibility points, and any required [settlement funds](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html). Eligibility to enter the pool is not an invitation to immigrate. Electrical engineers (NOC 21310) appear in the [STEM category list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html), subject to the current category criteria. If there is no skilled work experience, this route is not ready yet.

## United Kingdom

The [Skilled Worker visa](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job) needs a licensed sponsor, eligible occupation, and the higher of the general salary floor (**£41,700**) or the occupation's going rate. The [official occupation table](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-skilled-occupations) lists electrical engineering code 2123 with a **£47,100** standard going rate; exceptions and current rates must be checked for the exact offer. This is a tougher first target for a new graduate than Ireland/Germany. Budget for the [visa fee, health surcharge, and maintenance requirement](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/how-much-it-costs).

## Priority

Apply to Germany and Ireland in parallel. Use Canada as a plan if the experience/test/funds conditions are met. Pursue the UK when a qualifying sponsor offer is realistic. Avoid paying a recruiter for a promised permit; verify employers, contracts, and government fees on official portals.
