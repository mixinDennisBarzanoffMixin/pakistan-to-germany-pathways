# English-speaking alternatives

Checked 10 October 2026. These are skilled routes; willingness to do any work does not remove their permit rules.

## Ireland

**Best English-speaking parallel target.** Electrical engineers (SOC 2123) are on Ireland's [Critical Skills Occupations List](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/highly-skilled-eligible-occupations-list/critical%20skills%20occupations%20list.html). A [Critical Skills Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/) needs a genuine job offer for at least **two years**, a relevant degree/skills, and the applicable salary floor. The permit page gives **€40,904/year in 2026** for the degree-based Critical Skills category. Its recent-graduate reduction only applies within 12 months of graduation, so a November 2024 graduate no longer qualifies in October 2026. The [permit fee](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/fees/) is **€1,000**. A separate government remuneration overview shows €40,909, so verify the live permit form before relying on either amount.

**Sequence:** find an eligible employer, agree a contract and permit application (normally file at least 12 weeks before the intended start), obtain the permit, then apply from Pakistan for an [Irish long-stay D employment visa](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/employment-visa/) via AVATS. The single-entry [visa fee](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/preclearance-and-entry-visas-fees/) is €60. The visa checklist includes qualification evidence and six months of bank statements; an employment permit is not itself a visa. Register immigration permission after arrival. There is no general Critical Skills English-test requirement stated on the permit page, but employers assess English ability.

Search [JobsIreland](https://jobsireland.ie/en-US/job-seekers) and employer career pages for electrical, power, controls, automation, testing, and commissioning roles. If a role is eligible but not on the Critical Skills list, the [General Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/) may be an alternative; it generally needs a labour-market needs test and has a **€36,605** 2026 salary floor. [Cleaning occupations are on the ineligible list](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/ineligible-categories-of-employment/ineligible%20list%20of%20occupations%20.html), so they are not an ordinary permit path.

## Canada

[Federal Skilled Worker Express Entry](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/federal-skilled-workers.html) can be explored if he has **at least one year of continuous qualifying skilled work**, an approved language test at **CLB 7** or better, an Educational Credential Assessment for the foreign degree, enough eligibility points, and any required [settlement funds](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html). Eligibility to enter the pool is not an invitation to immigrate. Electrical engineers (NOC 21310) appear in the [STEM category list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html), subject to the current category criteria. If there is no skilled work experience, this route is not ready yet.

## United Kingdom

The [Skilled Worker visa](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job) needs a licensed sponsor, eligible job, and salary that meets both the general floor and that occupation's going rate. For a **new first-time applicant** in electrical engineering (SOC **2123**), [Appendix Skilled Occupations, Table 1](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-skilled-occupations) shows **£58,700/year** standard at 37.5 hours/week. Because he is **under 26 at application**, the [new-entrant rule](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/when-you-can-be-paid-less) can reduce that occupation's threshold to **£41,100/year** (70%, still above the £33,400 general floor); the discount has a maximum four-year stay. The **£47,100** figure in the same appendix is a transitional table for existing workers and is not the starting figure for this first overseas application. Salary is prorated for hours, so check the exact offer and code.

Self-reported C1 English does not satisfy visa evidence by itself. The current [English rule](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/knowledge-of-english) requires **B2 proof** in all four skills, through an approved secure test or an Ecctis assessment of an English-taught overseas degree. Search [GOV.UK Find a Job](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-job) and employer sites, then check the employer against the [licensed sponsor register](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers). After receiving a Certificate of Sponsorship, [apply online from Pakistan](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/apply-from-outside-the-uk) and obtain the required [TB certificate from an approved clinic](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinics-in-pakistan). Budget for the [visa fee, annual health surcharge, and maintenance funds](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/how-much-it-costs). [Cleaner roles are not eligible Skilled Worker occupations](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/skilled-worker-visa-eligible-occupations/skilled-worker-visa-eligible-occupations-and-codes).

## Priority

Apply to Germany and Ireland in parallel; add UK engineering vacancies at licensed sponsors while he remains under 26, since its reduced salary threshold is time-sensitive. Use Canada as a plan if the experience/test/funds conditions are met. Avoid paying a recruiter for a promised permit; verify employers, contracts, and government fees on official portals.
