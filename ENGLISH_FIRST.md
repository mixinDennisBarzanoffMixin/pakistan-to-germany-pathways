## Do this in the next seven days

1. Create a one-page English CV listing Air University BE, actual paid and unpaid electrical/site work separately, tools/software, English C1, Rawalpindi, passport status, and willingness to relocate for farm, maintenance, warehouse, or hospitality work. Never claim experience he lacks.
2. Treat UK seasonal as a **watchlist**, because current operator recruitment lists do not show an open Pakistan channel. Check the [government operator list](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seasonal-work-on-farms-guidance-for-workers) and each operator's own recruitment-country page periodically. Apply only if an operator explicitly opens recruitment for Pakistan. Do not pay for a sponsorship certificate or recruitment place. UK guidance says workers normally pay the visa and travel themselves.
3. Register at the [Pakistan Overseas Employment Corporation portal](https://jobs.oec.gov.pk/register). Search UAE/Saudi/Qatar for `electrician`, `electrical technician`, `maintenance`, `warehouse`, `construction`, `hotel`, and `cleaner`; apply only where he meets the posted criteria. Prioritize listings with employer-paid air ticket and accommodation. Verify any outside recruiter in the [BEOE licence list](https://beoe.gov.pk/list-of-oeps).
4. Search [Malta Jobsplus](https://jobsplus.gov.mt/) for `kitchen porter`, `dishwasher`, `cleaner`, `warehouse operative`, `maintenance technician`, and `electrical technician`. Ask upfront: “Can you apply for a Single Permit for an applicant still in Pakistan, and pay or advance the €600 permit fee, mandatory course, visa and flight?” The [Maltese authority](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/who-is-eligible/) says the employer must apply.
5. Keep a tracker: employer/operator, official link, role, date applied, reply, wage, accommodation, visa category, and who pays each cost. Raise money for a passport and documents first. **Do not book a flight or pay an intermediary on a promise.**

## Funding decision

If he can raise only small sums, **UAE employer-funded recruitment is the first funding fit**. **Malta is the English-speaking country to actively approach now** for longer-term work, but only a real employer willing to file the permit and cover/advance costs makes it feasible. UK seasonal is not an active Pakistan application channel in the operator lists checked above. There is no verified offer for this person in this project yet.

The [UK visa rules](https://www.gov.uk/seasonal-worker-visa/overview) allow only the sponsored seasonal job, not a second or permanent job. A seasonal stay should be planned as temporary earning and travel, with any later immigration application assessed on its own terms.
