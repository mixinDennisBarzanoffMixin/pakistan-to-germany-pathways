## English test decision

**Do not spend money until checking the English-medium letter.** His report that Air University taught the BE in English could let E-PiCo consider him without IELTS once the university issues official confirmation. STEPS currently lists an approved test for a non-native speaker, so he would need one to pursue both programmes unless STEPS confirms an exemption directly.

For STEPS and E-PiCo together, book **IELTS Academic on computer**, aiming for **at least 6.5 overall** (and at least 5.5 in each component for STEPS). The [British Council Pakistan fee page](https://www.britishcouncil.pk/exam/ielts/book-test) lists **PKR 75,600** for Islamabad. The [IDP Islamabad live test listing](https://ielts.idp.com/pakistan/test-dates/islamabad), checked 10 October 2026, showed the next IELTS Academic computer session **Sunday 11 October 2026, 1:00 pm**, AEO Islamabad, with seats shown at **PKR 75,600**. Availability can change without notice. [British Council Pakistan](https://www.britishcouncil.pk/exam/ielts/results) says computer IELTS results normally arrive in **1–2 days**. Standard Academic is the programme test; do not pay extra for UKVI solely for these Erasmus applications.
