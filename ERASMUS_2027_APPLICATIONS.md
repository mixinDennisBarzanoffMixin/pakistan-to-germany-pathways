Availability can change without notice. [British Council Pakistan](https://www.britishcouncil.pk/exam/ielts/results) says computer IELTS results normally arrive in **1–2 days**. Standard Academic is the programme test; do not pay extra for UKVI solely for these Erasmus applications.
